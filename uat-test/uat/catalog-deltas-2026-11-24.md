## What was not checked

- **No run.** The UAT suite was not executed; the comparison is static (spec source vs. catalog). A spec that is correct on paper may still fail, and expected texts were not verified against the live UI or translations.
- **Helper depth.** Support commands (`cypress/support/commands/*`) were read for what they assert, but backend behaviour behind GraphQL seeding (`scripts/baseline.mjs`, `scripts/claim-seed.mjs`) was taken from the code, not from a database.
- **Untagged tests.** 99 tests carry no OCM tag (mostly grievance, payment-plan, payment-point, payroll, payment-cycle, cash-transfer, claim; also `uat-social-programs-payroll.cy.js:294`, OP-3313). They were not matched to catalog cases; some may implement cases listed under (b) or under DUPLICATE/out-of-scope verdicts.
- **admin.cy.js** could not be loaded by the title collector (it uses `require`); a text scan found no OCM tag in it.
- **Cases not implemented** (out of scope or (b)) were not re-reviewed: their verdicts, reasons and proposed_steps were left as they were.
- **original_steps** were not compared with the specs (kept untouched by design), and `reason` texts were not refreshed (e.g. OCM-1230 still says OP-XXXX).
- **Menu paths** were corrected only where a spec asserts or navigates them; specs open most pages by URL (`cypress/support/routes.js`), so other menu hints are unverified against the pinned release menu (`uat/module-config/fe-core.release.json`).
- **TestLink** itself was not consulted; OCM-1230 still has to be created there.
- Per-case comparison was split across seven review passes (one per spec area) and merged; field changes were checked mechanically (only allowed fields, every implemented case covered exactly once), and OCM-1230 was spot-checked by hand against the spec, but not every one of the 296 rewrites was re-read individually.
